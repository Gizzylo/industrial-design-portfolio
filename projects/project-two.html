Curbside EV

Instead of relying on bulky, expensive standalone pedestals that clutter sidewalks, Curbside EV specializes in innovative units that mount directly onto existing utility or lighting posts. By leveraging existing municipal infrastructure, their technology slashes installation costs, minimizes civil construction, and accelerates how quickly cities can deploy equitable charging networks.
