A bit about me

I'm an industrial designer based in the Bay Area, working across product form, mechanism and manufacture. I came to design through making things — shop work first, then CAD, then the long middle part where a drawing meets a supplier and has to survive. Most of my work sits between a sketch and a production-ready part, which is where the interesting decisions are. I care about products that feel resolved in the hand and are honest about how they were made. Right now I'm looking for work where I can stay close to engineering rather than hand off renders.
