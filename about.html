About Cindy Lopez

Great hardware is rarely born entirely in CAD—it is figured out through physical prototyping, mechanical intuition, and shop-floor iteration. Based in the Bay Area, I design products with a focus on how internal mechanisms dictate external form and how components actually get manufactured. My work centers on the vital middle ground between a rough concept and a production-ready assembly, ensuring every object feels solid and resolved the second you hold it. When I step away from the workbench, that same curiosity takes over: in my free time, I’m an avid traveler and explorer.
