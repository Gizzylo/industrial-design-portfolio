Project Three

A short line naming the product and who it is for.

01 — Context

Where this started

Two or three sentences on the situation you were designing into: who the user is, what they are doing when they reach for this, and why existing options fall short.

02 — Research

What I looked at

Interviews, teardowns, benchmarking — whatever you actually did. Say how many people you spoke to and what surprised you.

03 — The problem

What needed solving

Land the insight in a sentence or two, then let the design question below carry it.

How might Project Three feel more considered in both function and form?

04 — Users

Who it is for

The person this is shaped around, and the moment of use that drove the decisions.

05 — Ideation

Exploring the form

Sketch directions and what each one was testing. Show the ones you rejected and say why.

06 — Development

Resolving it in CAD

Surfacing, internal layout, how the parts come together. Name the materials and the processes they imply.

07 — Prototyping

Making it real

Print iterations, mock-ups, the fit issue you only found in the hand.

08 — Details

The parts worth pointing at

The interface, the mechanism, the seam you moved to make it mouldable.

09 — Final design

Where it landed

The resolved product, shot from the angles that explain it.
