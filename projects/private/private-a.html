SBSA - School Bus Stop Arm

By equipping school buses with Hayden AI’s advanced mobile vision and artificial intelligence systems, this project provides a reliable, automated way to detect and capture motorists who illegally pass school buses while their stop arms are extended and red lights are flashing.
