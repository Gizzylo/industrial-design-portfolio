Root Spore Collector

This air sampler is engineered to provide continuous, reliable monitoring of airborn crop pathogens in agricultural enviroments. By capturing microscopic fungal spores-such as those responsible for powdery mildew-beforesymptoms appear, the device empowers growers with critical data to optimize treatment timing, protect yirlds, and reduce unecesary fungicide use.
